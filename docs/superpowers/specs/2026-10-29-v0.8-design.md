# v0.8: self and world in one learned motion code — design

## Intent

v0.7's MT cells sense motion they learned. But they cannot tell whether a motion was caused by the eye or by the world. Protocol 12 already separates the two, but with an engineered motor map and image registration.

Joscha Bach's point about the self-model: a mind knows which changes it causes. Following the project's direction (a virtual cortex, engineered parts replaced by learned ones), v0.8 makes that distinction inside the learned motion code. The eye's command is told to MT (the efference copy), and MT learns what each command does to its own cells.

The user delegated the design decisions ("its up to you"). All calls below are Claude's.

## Mechanism

- **The eye makes small saccades:** 8 view pixels (4 retinal pixels, inside the fine motion cells' reach), in 8 directions, from the sprite world of protocol 12.
- **The efference copy:**
  - Before each frame, the command just issued (0 = none) is given to MT.
  - An **efference map** E[c, m] holds, for each command c, the mean responsibility of each fine motion type m on the frames right after that command. It learns by a local delta rule (Hebbian averaging): E[c] ← E[c] + η (mean over places of r_m − E[c]).
  - A newborn's map is uniform: no command predicts anything.
- **Self-caused motion expected** for command c: v_self(c) = Σ_m E[c, m] · displacement(m). This is read from the same learned cells; there is no engineered motor map.
- **World motion** at each place: the sensed velocity minus v_self(c).

The same MT cells carry both. The self part is what the command explains, and the world part is what remains.

## Protocol 18 (written before measuring): self and world in learned motion

- **Worlds:** protocol 12's sprite world, 384 × 288, 2 moving objects; the view is 256 × 192, sent to the cortex at 128 × 96.
- **Training (efference map only; MT is the frozen v0.7 adult):** seeds 800–839, 600 frames each, a random small saccade every 5 frames (a direction chosen at random among those keeping the view inside the world).
- **Test:** seeds 8000–8019.
  - After 30 still frames, one saccade is made in a random direction.
  - **Commanded:** the command is told.
  - **Uncommanded:** the same retinal shift, with command 0.
  - For each, the world motion is the median over the view's central region of |sensed velocity − v_self|. It is divided by the true shift of 4 retinal pixels per frame, or about 5.7 for diagonals.
- **Conditions:**
  - adult efference map (trained);
  - newborn efference map (uniform);
  - both with the v0.7 MT adult.
- **Pass criteria:**
  - adult: commanded world motion fraction ≤ 0.3, and uncommanded ≥ 0.7;
  - newborn map: commanded ≥ 0.7 (it takes its own saccade for the world moving).
- **Reported:** the sensed velocity's direction against the saccade's, as a check that MT senses the shift at all.

## Non-goals

- Replacing protocol 12's registration in the scene graph. This is a demonstration in the cortex's own code; wiring it into the graph is a later step.
- Large saccades: those are beyond MT's reach.

## Constraints

As before:
- local rules only;
- heavy runs on flank;
- no attribution;
- failures reported;
- engineered parts labelled.

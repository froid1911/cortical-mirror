# v0.9: faster motion — design

## Intent

The v0.7 MT adult learned motion of one V1 position per frame in all eight directions, but motion of two positions only along three axes and one diagonal. UCF101 rarely shows faster motion. That limited protocol 18 to small saccades, and it likely limits real-video prediction. v0.9 widens the motion cells' speed range.

All calls are Claude's; the user delegated them, with the standing goal of working toward what would serve Joscha Bach.

## Changes

- **More motion cell types:** 32 per scale instead of 16, room for a second speed in every direction.
- **Faster motion in training:** half of the training streams skip every other frame (stride 2). That doubles the speeds they show, as a faster world would. Nothing else changes: same rule, same V1 adult, same two scales.
- **The MT adult is raised for 4 hours** on flank's GPU 1.

## Pre-registered runs (written before training)

- **Protocol 16** (motion cells), same stimuli and the amended criteria. One new measure with its own criterion, **fast coverage:** the number of compass sectors covered by types whose displacement is at least 1.5 V1 positions long. Pass: ≥ 6 (the v0.7 adult covered 4).
- **Protocol 17** (learned motion used) on 40 fresh UCF101 test clips: seed 3, excluding the clips of seeds 0, 1 and 2. Same criteria. v0.7's MT adult and optical flow are reported on the same clips.
- **Protocol 18** (self and world) at the **original saccade size of 8 view pixels** (2 V1 positions): training seeds 800–839, test seeds 8020–8039. Same criteria.

Failures are reported as failures.

# v0.6: predicting real video from moving parts — design

## Intent

v0.5's graph predicted UCF101 at 1.04 × the error of repeating the last frame; protocol 15 asks for below 0.95. The headroom measurement (`reports/scene/dev-headroom.json`, UCF101 validation) showed:

- carrying the last frame's motion forward is worse than repeating the frame, for every motion model;
- motion averaged over three frames and carried forward at half strength beats it by about 15%;
- an affine motion per region gets almost all of that: 0.85, against 0.84 for dense flow.

v0.6 therefore predicts the next frame from the motion of the scene graph's parts.

## Architecture (Claude's calls)

- **Motion sensing (innate):**
  - Dense optical flow between consecutive frames (Farnebäck), in the role of the motion-sensitive area MT.
  - It is given, not learned. Like the retina's normalisation, it is an innate front end, and that is declared.
- **Parts:**
  - Every object node in view is a part, its region being its mask.
  - Whatever no node claims is divided into 32 px blocks of background.
- **Motion per part:** an affine map fitted to the flow inside the part by least squares.
- **Prediction:**
  - Each part's affine motion is averaged over the last three frame pairs and carried forward at half strength.
  - The parts' motions are composed into one field, which moves the current frame into the predicted next frame.
- **Where the graph still decides:**
  - The parts come from the graph's nodes.
  - Each node's own appearance is not used: on real video it does not yet beat moving the frame itself.

The camera registration of v0.5 remains: it keeps the canvas and the nodes aligned.

## Protocol

Protocol 15 is unchanged. It is re-run for v0.6 on **40 test clips chosen with seed 1, excluding the 40 of the v0.5 run** (seed 0). The pass criterion stays: ratio below 0.95. The v0.5 condition (registration, normalised) is re-run on the same clips for comparison.

## Order of work

1. The part motion predictor, test-first.
2. Development on UCF101 validation (flank).
3. Protocol 15 on fresh test clips.
4. Release.

Permanence is diagnosed separately, case by case, before any further tracking change.

## Amendment, before any test measurement (2026-10-28)

- **Development result:** on 20 UCF101 validation clips, part motion predicts at 0.84 × the error of repeating the last frame, better on 90% of clips. v0.5's prediction scores 1.03 on the same clips.
- **Permanence, diagnosed case by case** on development seeds 3100–3119:
  - knowing the screen in advance does not help (13 of 20 kept either way);
  - lost objects lag about 9–10 px when fully hidden, then 17–29 px when they reappear: the node's velocity decayed while the object slid behind the screen.
  - The cause: velocity was still updated from partial views, where the best fit lags.
  - The rule adopted: an established node's motion (15 frames or older), like its shape, is learned only from complete views. That gave 16 of 20 kept, at first at a cost to discovery. The final development numbers are in `reports/scene/README.md`.
- **Pre-registered runs for v0.6:**
  - protocol 15 on 40 fresh test clips (seed 1, excluding the v0.5 run's seed 0);
  - protocol 9 on seeds 3040–3059;
  - protocol 13 on seeds 6020–6039;
  - protocols 8, 10 and 12 re-run on their original seeds, to check that nothing regressed.

  The criteria are unchanged.
